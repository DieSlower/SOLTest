# SDD 25: Map alignment and orbit-line shimmer (bug fix)

**Issue:** [#25](https://github.com/DieSlower/SOLTest/issues/25). In the jump map, planets did not sit on their orbit lines, and the orbit lines and markers shimmered while the map camera moved. A bug fix, not a feature: no grill-me (the user scheduled it as its own commit before #20). Related: SDD 17 (orbit lines), SDD 3 (jump map).

## 1. Root causes

### 1.1 Misalignment: the HUD and the renderer used different FOVs

Every screen-space projection the game does itself treats the camera FOV as **horizontal across the whole viewport**: `ASOLFlightHud::BuildView` (map body icons and labels, target bracket, velocity markers, the destination-pick overlay), `USOLMapModeSubsystem::GetVerticalFovRad` (map icon LOD and picking ray), `ASOLBodyVisuals` exposure (horizontal FOV; its vertical extent from the camera's 16:9 aspect). The HUD comment said "the local player keeps the X FOV by default". It does not: the engine default is `[/Script/Engine.LocalPlayer] AspectRatioAxisConstraint=AspectRatio_MaintainYFOV` (`BaseEngine.ini`), under which `FMinimalViewInfo` keeps the vertical FOV that the FOV value gives **at the camera's 16:9 aspect ratio**. So the 3D view (bodies' meshes, orbit lines) and the HUD (body icons, brackets) agreed only in a 16:9 viewport. In any other viewport (the editor's PIE viewport, a 16:10 or 21:9 monitor, a resized window) the 3D view is scaled about the screen centre relative to the HUD, so every icon off-centre sits off its orbit line (at 5:4: 39-156 px in the `-SOLSmokeMapAlign` views; the target bracket is also smaller than the planet's mesh).

The line placement itself was verified correct: on the CPU (SampleOrbit + PlaceLinePointCm + the body's render placement, 0.000 px in every view) and on the GPU (16:9 screenshots of every view: Moon, Earth near and far, Io, Jupiter, Saturn, Neptune sit on their highlighted lines).

### 1.2 Shimmer while moving: the lines went through TSR without motion

`M_SOLOrbitLine` was drawn in the `AfterDOF` translucency pass, which TSR composites into its history. The lines are placed by world position offset and have no velocity, so TSR reprojected their history with the motion of whatever opaque pixel lies behind them (usually the sky: camera rotation only). In the map the render origin re-snaps to the camera every frame it moves, so every object moves in render space each frame; the lines' history landed in the wrong place, was blended or rejected, and the sub-pixel TAA jitter on 1-2 px analytic lines showed through. Motion blur also smeared them with the background's velocity. Same mechanism as the CLAUDE.md render-origin motion-vector tech-debt item, here for the line ribbons.

The canvas markers (icons, labels, brackets, pick overlay) are drawn after the frame and were never temporally filtered; their apparent swim was relative to the lagging lines.

## 2. Changes

1. **`Config/DefaultEngine.ini`:** `[/Script/Engine.LocalPlayer] AspectRatioAxisConstraint=AspectRatio_MaintainXFOV`. The FOV is now horizontal across the viewport at every aspect ratio, which is what all the projection code already assumed. At 16:9 nothing changes. Elsewhere the horizontal view is kept (a taller window shows more vertically, a wider one less) and HUD, icons, picking, star sizes and exposure match the 3D view. Chosen over rewriting each projection site to read the engine's matrix because the assumption is spread over several systems and is also what the docs state (60° horizontal map camera, 90-110° chase camera). Cost: on ultrawide displays the vertical view shrinks (at 21:9 the 90° chase camera shows about 46° vertically instead of 59°); taller displays gain vertical view. `ASOLBodyVisuals::UpdateExposure` took its vertical extent from the camera's 16:9 aspect ratio (right only under the old rule), and now takes it from the viewport's aspect ratio.
2. **`M_SOLOrbitLine` translucency pass: After Motion Blur** (`Tools/OrbitLines/create_orbit_line_assets.py`, applied to the asset). That pass is unjittered, composited after TSR and kept out of TSR's history, and is not motion-blurred. The lines already anti-alias themselves analytically (`SOLOL_LineCoverage`, dash box filter), so they need no temporal AA. Depth test was already off; the pixel shader's scene-depth test and analytic sphere occlusion are unchanged (scene depth is still bound in that pass). The lines now draw over all other translucency by pass order (before: by `ORBIT_LINE_TRANSLUCENCY_SORT_PRIORITY`, which now only orders lines among themselves); same visible result (SDD 17 Q7). **Trade-off:** below 100% screen percentage (the engine's display-based default on high-resolution screens, or dynamic resolution) this pass renders at render resolution and is bilinearly upsampled after TSR instead of being reconstructed by TSR, so the lines look softer (checked at `r.ScreenPercentage 50`: same width, visibly blurrier edges and dashes). If that matters, the line width and coverage could be computed in output pixels, or the pass could be rendered at output resolution. Cost: one extra render-resolution colour target composited in the motion-blur pass (or one full-resolution composition pass when motion blur is off); not profiled, expected well under 0.1 ms.
3. **`-SOLSmokeMapAlign`** (`Map/SOLMapAlignSmoke`, `SOL::CommandLine::SMOKE_MAP_ALIGN`, `SOL::MAP_ALIGN_SMOKE_TOLERANCE_PX` = 1 px; run it with a non-16:9 viewport, e.g. `-game -windowed -ResX=1280 -ResY=1024`, since at 16:9 both FOV rules agree and it logs a warning): opens the map with J and, for 8 views (Moon around Earth, Earth near and far, Jupiter close, Earth around the Sun, Io around Jupiter, Saturn, Neptune), highlights the target (checking that the highlighted line is the target's own slot), aims the camera, and checks that the target's icon (projected as the HUD projects it) lies within 1 px of its orbit line (placed as the shader places it, projected with the renderer's own view-projection matrix from `ULocalPlayer::GetProjectionData`). Screenshots per view and one mid-orbit (fixed frame count, so repeatable). It fails at 5:4 with the old constraint (5 of 8 views off by 39-156 px) and passes with the new one (26/26, all within 0.17 px).
4. **Test `SOLTest.ViewProjection.FovIsHorizontalAtEveryAspect`** (`Tests/ViewProjectionTest.cpp`): the configured local-player constraint is MaintainXFOV, and the engine's projection (`FMinimalViewInfo::CalculateProjectionMatrixGivenViewRectangle`) has the HUD's pinhole scales (X `1/tan(FOV/2)`, Y that times W/H) for 60° and 90° at 16:9, 5:4, 16:10, 21:9 and a portrait viewport.

No gameplay value changed. `GAME_MECHANICS.md` gains the smoke and the FOV-is-horizontal-at-any-aspect note; the in-game text already says the FOVs are horizontal and needed no change.

## 3. The white line (question in the issue thread)

Identified from `-game` screenshots: with orbit lines on (O), the white-grey dashed lines that run through both the flight view and the map, not attached to any visible body, are the **orbits of the 50 largest named asteroids** (`SOLAsteroidBelt::RealAsteroids`, drawn in `SOL::ORBIT_LINE_MINOR_COLOR` (0.42, 0.42, 0.5, alpha 0.12), against the blue planet and moon lines; dense around the inner system in the map). Other white elements: the thin white circle around the screen centre in the flight view is the virtual joystick's range ring (HUD, `ASOLFlightHud::DrawJoystick`), and the cyan disc and radius line in the map are the destination-pick overlay after a left click/drag. The P key's predicted path is still a no-op and draws nothing.

## 4. Open points

- Not verified by eye on the user's own display; the PIE viewport is the likely place they saw it. A one-frame A/B of TSR on and off during an orbit sweep showed no static offset, so the shimmer fix is argued from the renderer's pass order (TSR history, jitter, motion blur) and checked by screenshot, not by a frame-difference metric.
- Other render-space effects without motion vectors (bolts, pooled combat effects) still go through TSR; that remains the existing CLAUDE.md tech-debt item.

## Amendment 1: date-dependent 'Earth on line' failure (smoke artifact)

**Symptom.** At some sim times (e.g. the real date on 2026-10-28 around 06:58 UTC) `-SOLSmokeMapAlign` failed 25/26: in the Earth close-up (focus Earth, 4e7 m) the icon was measured 290-400 px off its line, with and without `sol.Hologram 0`; at `-SOLSimDate=2026-10-28T01:09:00Z` it passed.

**Root cause: the smoke's CPU mirror, not the game.** The mirror projected each line vertex and **dropped every chord with an endpoint behind the camera** (`clip.W <= 0`) instead of clipping it. Seen from 4e7 m, Earth's line chords (8192-segment mirror: ~1.15e8 m; the real line: ~1,522 segments, ~6.2e8 m) are longer than the camera distance, so the chord running through Earth often starts behind the camera. Earth moves one mirror chord about every hour, so whether that chord survived depended on the time of day; when it was dropped, the nearest remaining chord was hundreds of pixels away. The rasterizer clips such a chord at the near plane, so the drawn line was always right: the failing run's screenshot shows the highlighted line through Earth's centre. **Not user-visible.** The other candidates were ruled out: body and line come from the same elements at the same instant (the Earth row is the Earth-Moon barycentre for both), the highlighted slot check passed, and the line's rebuild/shader-rotation drift is bounded by 5% of the body radius (SDD 17).

**Fix (smoke and tests only, no game change).**
- `FSOLMapAlignSmoke::ScreenDistanceToClipPolylinePx` clips each clip-space chord to `W >= 1 cm` before projecting, as the rasterizer does.
- The mirror now samples the line with `ASOLOrbitLineVisuals`' segment count (`SegmentCountFor(a, radius)`, not a fixed 8192), from the current elements; the actor's own rebuild drift (built elements plus a shader rotation, up to 5% of the radius, SDD 17) is not mirrored. Near-plane W is 1 cm, approximately the engine's 10 cm near clip. The tolerance becomes `MAP_ALIGN_SMOKE_TOLERANCE_PX` plus that line's designed chord error (`ChordSagittaM`, at most 5% of the body radius) projected at the body's distance (Earth at 4e7 m in a 1000 px wide view: ~7.9 px against a 138 px disc radius; the FOV mismatch it exists to catch was 39-156 px). Each check logs its tolerance.
- New tests `SOLTest.MapAlign.ChordCrossingCameraPlaneIsClipped` (the clipping helper) and `SOLTest.MapAlign.BodyOnItsLineAtAnyDate` (every registry body within its line's chord error, every 29 min over two days and every 2.5 years across 1950-2050).
- Smoke at 1000 x 800 passed 26/26 at the real date and at `-SOLSimDate` 2026-10-28T03:40Z, 05:10Z, 2027-03-14T12:00Z and 2031-07-02T19:25Z (Earth close-up 1.6-6.3 px against 7.9 px).
