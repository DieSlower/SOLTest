# SOLTest — Game Mechanics, Controls & Tunables

A catalog of every player-facing mechanic, control binding and tunable value, with the exact numbers and the source location that implements it. Consult it first when reasoning about gameplay. **Keep it in sync** whenever a mechanic, binding or value changes, and update the in-game Controls / Info / About screen in the same change (see `CLAUDE.md`, "Game mechanics catalog & in-game info").

Status column: **Designed** = decided in an SDD, not built; **Built** = implemented and tested. Source references are filled in as parts land.

---

hint line always show the current keys.

`-SOLSmokeInput` drives the real input pipeline with injected key, mouse and wheel events (`Ship/SOLShipSmokeInput.cpp`; issue #19 adds C for manual / assisted, B recenter, the wheel click turning mouse steering off so the mouse does not move the stick, Space hold steering and recentering, Left Shift thrust up, Tab boost on and off, and an autopilot cancelled to ASSISTED by W, with screenshots of the MANUAL, ASSISTED and AUTO labels); both log a PASS/FAIL per check and quit (`-SOLSmokeInput` after a screenshot). `-SOLSmokeHud` drives the HUD keys (T, O, P) and the F3 panel (panel keys through Slate), checks that W does nothing while the panel is open, and ends with two screenshots (HUD over Earth, orbit lines from 3 AU above the Sun; the lines are forced on without touching the saved setting) (`Map/SOLMapSmoke.cpp`). `-SOLSmokeMapPick` drives the destination pick (click a body icon, drag, release, Shift height up and back, Shift+click, Enter, X, then the same from empty space with the ship as reference, then one drag on the real OS-cursor path checking that the OS cursor ends on the map cursor after mouse-up and the next move does not jump, then close and reopen), logs a PASS/FAIL per check and takes screenshots of the disc, the height guide line and the destination marker (`Map/SOLMapPickSmoke.cpp`); ## 2. Flight

| Mechanic | Value / rule | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Flight model | Issue #18 Newtonian, thrust-limited core (`SOLNewtonian::StepNewtonian`): nothing writes velocity directly, both modes only command thrust and torque inside the envelope, and gravity applies in both. **Assisted** (C toggles, default): rate-command rotation plus a velocity hold toward reference + thrust x cap, clamped to the pilot's sustained 1.5 G with gravity compensation served first (SDD 18 Amendment 3), then allocated to the thrusters. **Manual**: keys are thruster fractions (a diagonal fires several thrusters), the stick is torque, spin persists until Z. The ship is a Mass entity stepped on real time in substeps of at most 1/30 s (at most 16 per frame); a frame longer than 0.5 s (a hitch) is clamped to 0.5 s for both the sim clock and the ship, so the whole universe slows uniformly | Built (1b), `Flight/SOLFlight.cpp`, `Ship/SOLShipFlightProcessor.cpp` |
| Speed cap | Relative to the reference frame; starts at 1000 m/s; wheel steps x10^0.1, clamped 1 m/s to 0.5c (149,896 km/s); the F3 panel sets it digit by digit or from a body's orbital speed | Built (1b wheel, 1c F3), `Ship/SOLShipPawn.cpp`, `UI/SOLSpeedPanelWidget.cpp`, `SOLConstants.h` |
| Assist response | Velocity hold time constant 0.75 s, never above the sustained 1.5 G (14.7 m/s²) of thrust including gravity compensation: 1,000 m/s takes ~68 s in free space; a body heavier than 1.5 G (Jupiter) cannot be hovered over; boost doubles the target speed | Built (#18), `Flight/SOLFlight.cpp` |
| Thrust envelope | Per-axis box: forward 3 G (29.4 m/s²), reverse 1.5 G, lateral 2 G, vertical 2 G; boost x3 on every axis; whole-ship cap 9 G (88.3 m/s²) | Built (#18), `Flight/SOLNewtonianFlight.h` |
| Rotation | Angular acceleration 4 / 3 / 3 rad/s² (roll / pitch / yaw), rate limits 120 / 90 / 90 °/s, spin clamp 360 °/s, spherical inertia, quaternion (no gimbal lock). Assisted: rate command (deadbeat, bounded). Manual: torque, spin persists. Stop spin (Z) in both | Built (#18), `Flight/SOLNewtonianFlight.cpp` |
| Pilot G | G-load = proper acceleration (thrust + resting surface contact; gravity and rotation are not felt) / 9.80665. Sustained 1.5 G (no drain), combat max 9 G. Above 1.5 G the meter drains at ((G - 1.5) / 7.5)² / 10 s per second (empty in 10 s at 9 G, 27.8 s at 6 G, 250 s at 3 G), recovers 0.05/s at or below 1.5 G (20 s). HUD: G-load and meter bar above the speed block; manual flight warns "G LIMIT EXCEEDED" over 1.5 G; meter <= 0.25 shows "G CRITICAL". Vision: tunnel vignette, desaturation and darkening from meter 0.4 to 0. At 0: G-LOC blackout (screen black, no player input, the ship coasts) for at least 5 s and until the meter is back to 0.2; while unconscious the meter always recovers, at least 0.02/s whatever the load, so a blackout under a constant heavy load lasts 10 s, then the pilot wakes, drains and blacks out again. After 30 s in a row depleted (meter <= 0.25 or blacked out) under a load above 1.5 G the HUD shows "LETHAL G" (warning only: no death until a damage system exists). A jump arrival or a new ship resets the meter to full. The meter is not saved | Built (#18), `Flight/SOLNewtonianFlight.cpp`, `Ship/SOLShipFlightProcessor.cpp`, `UI/SOLFlightHud.cpp`, `Ship/SOLShipPawn.cpp` |
| Flight modes | Three modes (issue #19, `Flight/SOLFlightMode.cpp`, owned by `USOLShipSubsystem`): MANUAL, ASSISTED and AUTO (the autopilot; it flies with assist semantics, so a frame where its order disappears falls into assisted flight). C switches MANUAL <-> ASSISTED and leaves AUTO for ASSISTED. AUTO is entered through `USOLShipSubsystem::EngageAutopilot` (the console `sol.Autopilot`; the interaction menus in #22) or `EngageAutopilotPoint` (a route waypoint, #20: Enter on the map, Resume) and every exit lands in ASSISTED: arrival, `sol.Autopilot Off`, C, a jump arrival, a respawn, or manual input. **Cancel rule:** in AUTO, any thrust axis, roll or gated stick component above 0.001, or stop spin held, cancels to ASSISTED in the same frame; boost, the speed cap, targeting, warp, the M / L locks, free-look and the steering keys do not. Input already held when AUTO engages is latched per channel (each thrust direction, roll direction, stick direction and stop spin): only those channels are ignored, each until it is released, and any other input cancels at once.
